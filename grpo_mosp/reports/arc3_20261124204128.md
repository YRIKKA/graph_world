# ARC-AGI-3 run 20261124204128

## Methods

The pinned Tufa runner evaluated the frozen Qwen3.8-27B 4-bit checkpoint with Transformers and bitsandbytes. The comparison crossed direct play with Duck’s tool policy and low with medium reasoning effort. Each condition played the same three easy games (SU15, KA59, LP85), one pass per game, with one game active at a time. The local generation deadline was 230 seconds, the client timeout was 240 seconds, and each level attempt had a two-hour limit with up to two recovery retries. Duck cleared KA59’s first level at medium effort and SU15’s first level at low effort. The native runner recorded partial RHAE of 0.96% and 1.59% for those passes, respectively. All 12 passes ended in errors and the comparison summary had zero scored passes and no aggregate RHAE. Ten passes hit the generation deadline. The other two ended on an invalid mouse action (direct, medium, SU15) and exhausted level retries (direct, low, KA59). Duck’s level clears are an early signal. The high failure rate and one pass per game leave both policy and reasoning-effort effects uncertain. ## Next steps

- Replay prompts that hit the deadline and measure queue time, generation speed, and tokens before cutoff. A matched vLLM pilot could test whether faster decoding reduces deadline failures with the same checkpoint and request budgets.
- Test a single correction attempt for malformed coordinates and report its effect separately from level recovery.
- Repeat paired passes across more games and seeds once inference is stable. Report completion rates and failure reasons alongside RHAE and level clears.
